One Hydra delegate for every host
==================================

This branch of [georgimitkovvfx-bit/cycles](https://github.com/georgimitkovvfx-bit/cycles) (a fork of
[blender/cycles](https://projects.blender.org/blender/cycles)) is Blender's Cycles with one Hydra render delegate
(`src/hydra`) for every host: Houdini (Solaris, husk), Pixar's usdview, Blender (the hdcycles-blender add-on, for
USD Nodes' Stage View, Live and Render node) and `cycles_usd_render`. Since 2026-11-26 it is the fork's `main` and
the master copy of our changes; the patch series in
[hdcycles-blender](https://github.com/georgimitkovvfx-bit/hdcycles-blender) `patches/` is frozen.

Two upstreams, both credited and both merged as they move:

* **Official Cycles** (`main`, blender/cycles; Apache-2.0). The base is `main` of 2026-11-16 (`6ea3d9bcd`, version
  5.4 in development).
* **hdCycles-h22** by Devanshu Tak ([projects.blender.org/devanshutak25/hdCycles-h22](https://projects.blender.org/devanshutak25/hdCycles-h22),
  Apache-2.0; itself based on [cjhosken/hdCycles](https://projects.blender.org/cjhosken/hdCycles)): the Houdini 22
  delegate this branch starts from (`92f394a1e`, branch `linux/houdini22` of
  [georgimitkovvfx-bit/hdcycles-h22](https://github.com/georgimitkovvfx-bit/hdcycles-h22) with our Linux fixes).
  Its README below describes the Houdini side (package, LOPs, VOPs, Material Builder).

Why h22 is the base, and the inventory of both delegates: USD Nodes `docs/27a_cycles_inventory.md`, ADR 0075 /
0076 (in [usdnodes](https://github.com/georgimitkovvfx-bit/usdnodes)).

## Differences from official Cycles

### From hdCycles-h22 (everything it has)

* **`src/hydra`** (+15 000 lines) and **`src/hydra/houdini/`** (the Houdini package: Cycles render settings, World
  and Sun & Sky LOPs, USD Cycles Material Builder with Cycles' shader VOPs, translators; generated by hython at
  install). Materials (Cycles networks, MaterialX, OSL, UsdPreviewSurface fixes), primvars of every type, curves
  of every basis, Gaussian splats, TetMesh, NURBS, generative procedurals, per-light settings, geometry lights,
  world materials, cameras (panoramic, lens OSL, rolling shutter), motion blur of curves, points, instancers and
  volumes, render settings as `cycles:*` with Karma's as fallbacks, primvar and shader AOVs, Cryptomatte, USD
  render passes, baking, the re-sync scene index, DLSS.
* **Cycles' core** (57 files, 24 of them in the kernel): nested dielectrics, baking per object and pass, UV tangents,
  volume velocity blur, Embree back faces of curves and points, OSL fixes, the portal crash on the GPU, display
  update interval, images reloaded when changed on disk. The kernel's data layout differs from Blender's: **GPU
  kernels must be built from this tree** (Blender's official kernels do not match).

## Builds and tests

Built by hdcycles-blender's `build.sh` (its README): the Blender 5.2 and 5.3 add-ons against Blender's own
libraries, Houdini 22 (`build.sh houdini`), Pixar's USD 26.08 (`build.sh usdview`), CUDA kernels for
`sm_75;sm_86;sm_89;sm_120;compute_75` (CUDA 13: Turing and newer). A change is done when every test passes on
every build:

* `make test` here: Cycles' unit tests and the delegate's render tests (`src/hydra/test/run_tests.py`: a dome's
  radiance, texture colour spaces, MaterialX, light and shadow links, implicit shapes and units, curves). GitHub's CI
  runs them on Linux (macOS and Windows experimental) once the repository has the secret `CI_ENABLE`;
* on our machines, hdcycles-blender's `test_all.sh` (`CYCLES_SRC_DIR=<checkout>`): every build, the render tests on
  the CPU and CUDA, USD Nodes' Blender suite on 5.2 and 5.3, `houdini_test.sh`, `usdview_test.sh` and the 15 husk
  scripts against Karma;
* for the Houdini GUI (LOPs, Material Builder), a check by hand.

## Upgrading

Official Cycles and hdCycles-h22 both move fast. Merge each, never let them pile up:

1. `git fetch upstream` (blender/cycles) and `git fetch h22-upstream` (h22's repository).
2. On a branch from `main`: `git merge upstream/main` (or a release tag), then h22's new commits
   (`git merge h22-upstream/<branch>`). Resolve conflicts keeping both sides' intent; our changes above are the
   ones to look after.
3. Rebuild every target (`build.sh configure build install deploy` for 5.2 and 5.3, `houdini`, `usdview`) and run
   every test above. Fix, with a test for each fix.
4. Fast-forward `main`, tag `hdcycles-<yyyy.mm.dd>`, push both. Record the merge in USD Nodes' `docs/PROGRESS.md`.
